### License, Deposit & Passport Requirements
*Inquiries about International Driving Permits (IDP), security deposits, and passport retention.*

**Common Questions Asked by Customers:**
- Do I need an International Driving Permit (IDP) to rent a bike in Thailand?
- What is the security deposit requirement?
- Do I have to leave my original passport as a deposit?

**Verified Standard Response:**
> An International Driving Permit (IDP Class A for motorcycles) or valid motorcycle license is required by Thai traffic law. For security deposit, you can either leave ฿1,000 - ฿5,000 THB cash (depending on model) OR leave your passport copy with deposit cash.
